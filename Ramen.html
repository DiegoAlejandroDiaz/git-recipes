Ramen

Description

Ramen is a beloved Japanese noodle soup consisting of wheat noodles served in a flavorful, savory broth made from pork, chicken, or dashi, traditionally topped with sliced pork, soft-boiled eggs, and fresh scallions.

Ingredients

  • Ramen noodles
  • Chicken or pork broth (or dashi stock)
  • Soy sauce (or miso paste / tare)
  • Chashu pork (sliced braised pork belly)
  • Ajitsuke Tamago (ramen soft-boiled egg)
  • Green onions (scallions)
  • Garlic
  • Fresh ginger
  • Sesame oil
  • Noris (seaweed sheets)
  • Menma (seasoned bamboo shoots)

Steps

  1. In a medium pot, heat sesame oil over medium heat. Sauté minced garlic and grated ginger for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  2. Pour in the broth along with soy sauce or miso paste to create the savory soup base, then bring to a gentle simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  3. In a separate pot, boil water and cook the ramen noodles according to package instructions (usually 2-3 minutes). Drain well.
  4. Divide the cooked noodles evenly into deep serving bowls.
  5. Ladle the hot broth over the noodles in each bowl.
  6. Arrange the sliced chashu pork, halved soft-boiled eggs, green onions, and bamboo shoots neatly on top of the noodles.
  7. Tuck a sheet of nori seaweed into the side of the bowl and serve immediately while hot.
