Paella

Description

Paella is a iconic Spanish rice dish originating from Valencia, cooked in a wide, shallow pan to create a flavorful saffron-infused rice layer with a crispy bottom crust known as socarrat.

Ingredients

  • Bomba rice (or short-grain rice)
  • Chicken thighs or seafood (shrimp, mussels, squid)
  • Saffron threads
  • Chicken or seafood stock
  • Olive oil
  • Grated tomatoes
  • Red bell pepper
  • Green beans (or garrofó beans)
  • Garlic
  • Sweet paprika
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Lemon wedges

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a wide paella pan over medium heat. Add the chicken or seafood and cook until browned, then remove seafood and set aside.
  2. In the same pan, sauté the red bell pepper, green beans, and minced garlic until tender.
  3. Stir in the grated tomatoes, sweet paprika, and saffron, cooking for a few minutes to build the sofrito base.
  4. Add the Bomba rice to the pan and stir for 1-2 minutes to coat the grains with the sofrito.
  5. Pour in the warm stock, season with salt, and bring to a boil. Do not stir the rice after this point.
  6. Lower the heat and let it simmer uncovered for about 15-18 minutes until the liquid is mostly absorbed.
  7. Arrange the browned seafood or reserved meat on top during the last 5 minutes of cooking.
  8. Increase the heat to high for 1-2 minutes to form the crispy bottom rice layer (socarrat), then remove from heat.
  9. Cover the pan with a clean towel and let it rest for 5 minutes before serving with fresh lemon wedges.
