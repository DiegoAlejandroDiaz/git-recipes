Cazuela

Description

A cazuela (or cazuela de mariscos when made with seafood) is a traditional, rich Latin American stew cooked and served in a earthenware or clay pot (also called a cazuela).

Ingredients

  • Shrimp
  • Squid rings
  • Octopus
  • Mussels and clams
  • Firm white fish fillets
  • Coconut milk
  • Fish or seafood stock
  • Diced tomatoes
  • Yellow or red onion
  • Scallions
  • Bell peppers
  • Garlic
  • White wine
  • Heavy cream
  • Cumin
  • Achiote or paprika
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Fresh parsley
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Lime wedges

Steps

  1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat some oil over medium heat. Add the diced onions, bell peppers, and scallions. Sauté until softened.
  2. Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
  3. Pour in the white wine and let it simmer for a couple of minutes to reduce slightly.
  4. Add the diced tomatoes, coconut milk, and seafood stock. Stir in the cumin and achiote or paprika. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
  5. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer and let it cook for about 10-15 minutes to allow the flavors to meld together.
  6. Add the firm white fish fillets, shrimp, squid rings, octopus, mussels, and clams to the pot. Cover and cook until the seafood is cooked through and the mussels and clams have opened (about 5-7 minutes).
  7. Stir in the heavy cream and let it heat through for a couple of minutes.
  8. Remove from heat and garnish with fresh cilantro and parsley.
  9. Serve hot with lime wedges on the side for squeezing over the stew.
